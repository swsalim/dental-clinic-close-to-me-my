# Does the site give medical or dental advice?

No. The directory and its guides are general information, not medical advice. For diagnosis, treatment choices and prices, consult a registered dentist. In an emergency in Malaysia, go to a hospital emergency department or call 999. See the [urgent visits guide](../articles/dental-clinic-open-now.md).

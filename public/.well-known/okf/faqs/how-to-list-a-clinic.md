# How do I list my dental clinic?

Go to [the submit page](https://www.dentalclinicclosetome.my/submit), enter your name, email and the clinic's Google Maps URL, and pay the one-time RM99 fee. The listing is reviewed within 24 hours. See [clinic listings](../concepts/clinic-listings.md).

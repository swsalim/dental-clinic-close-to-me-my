# What is dentalclinicclosetome.my?

It is Dental Clinics Malaysia, an online directory of dental clinics and dentists across Malaysia. You can search by state, city or service and compare clinic details such as services, ratings, opening hours and contact information. It is built by Yuurrific. See the [overview](../index.md).

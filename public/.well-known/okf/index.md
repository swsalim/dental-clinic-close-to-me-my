# Dental Clinics Malaysia

Dental Clinics Malaysia (dentalclinicclosetome.my) is an online directory of dental clinics and dentists in Malaysia. Patients can browse clinics by state and city, compare listed services, reviews, opening hours and contact details, and find individual dentists. The site is a directory with no physical premises; it does not provide dental treatment or medical advice.

As of 20 October 2026 the homepage shows 1,829 listed clinics, 197 dental professionals and 11 states. These counts come from the live database and change over time.

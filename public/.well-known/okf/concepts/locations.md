# Locations covered

As of 20 October 2026, 11 state-level pages have listed clinics and are in the sitemap: Johor, Kedah, Kuala Lumpur, Malacca, Negeri Sembilan, Penang, Perak, Perlis, Sabah, Sarawak and Selangor. Each state page links to city or area pages, for example `/sarawak/kuching`.

State pages with no listed clinics yet (such as Kelantan, Labuan, Pahang, Putrajaya and Terengganu) exist but are marked noindex and left out of the sitemap until clinics are added.

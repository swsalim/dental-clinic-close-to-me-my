# Listing and advertising for clinic owners

- **Paid listing:** a one-time fee of RM99. The owner submits a Google Maps URL for the clinic; the site fills in name, address and hours. Listings are reviewed within 24 hours and include a dofollow link to the clinic website. Source: [https://www.dentalclinicclosetome.my/submit](https://www.dentalclinicclosetome.my/submit).
- **Advertising:** featured placement is offered at `/advertise-with-us`; contact the site for details.
- **Contact:** hello@dentalclinicclosetome.my.

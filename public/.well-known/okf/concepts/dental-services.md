# Dental service pages

Clinics are grouped under 17 service pages at `/services/{service}`: general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, pediatric dentistry, orthodontics, periodontics, endodontics, oral surgery, prosthodontics, dental implants, emergency dentistry, dental X-rays, teeth whitening, Invisalign, root canal, braces, veneers and wisdom tooth extraction.

A service page lists clinics that offer the treatment with their location and rating. It does not give prices or treatment advice; patients should confirm services and fees with the clinic.
